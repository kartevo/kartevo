# Kartevo

## Check here before you check out.

Kartevo is a consumer shopping platform for product discovery, shopping collections, and personal budget tracking. We’re building a calmer way to organize what catches your eye and give yourself time to decide.

- **Discover and organize:** save favorites and group products into shopping collections.
- **Pause before purchasing:** optional 3-day Holds give you time to consider a purchase.
- **Track your shopping budget:** keep personal purchase records and see how recorded spending fits your budget.

Visit **[kartevo.com](https://kartevo.com)** to learn more.

This is Kartevo’s official GitHub profile. Our shopping platform is under active development; feature availability may vary.
